Project K — B2B Platform  
Statement of Work (SOW)  
1. Overview  
This document defines the production -ready requirements for Project K, a next -generation AI -
powered eLearning platform designed for higher education institutions across Tamil Nadu. The 
system t ransforms traditional static academic content into AI -driven learning experiences and 
includes an integrated AI Interview module.  
 
The platform is built on a B2B institutional model. Knotopian (Super Admin) manages affiliated 
universities and colleges. Eac h college is administered by  Admin &  HODs /Faculty. Students 
access learning content, AI tutoring, assessments, and interview preparation through a 
dedicated student portal.  
 
2. Vision  
To become the primary AI -powered academic companion for every college s tudent in Tamil 
Nadu , transforming raw academic content into fully structured, a i-driven learning experiences 
while enabling on -demand personalised tutoring and interview readiness.  
 
3. Product Scope  
The platform encompasses the following functional doma ins: 
 
• Onboarding of affiliated universities and their member colleges  
• Super Admin Portal — content upload, AI course generation, university/college 
management  
• College Admin Portal — content upload, AI course generation, multi -role access for  
admin,  HOD and  Faculty /SME.  
• Student Portal — Classroom (AI video lectures) + QnA AI Tutor + Assessments  
• Content -to-Learning Automation: raw academic documents to AI video lessons  
• English as the primary instruction language; Tamil language support for QnA interactions  
• Desktop web browser as the primary delivery platform  
• AI-generated MCQ knowledge checks with auto -evaluation  
• Analytics dashboards for Super Admin and College Admin  
• Student leaderboard

4. Business Model  
The platform operates on a B2B institutional licensing model. Knotopian contracts directly with 
universities and colleges. The platform is not consumer -facing from a billing perspective , 
institutions pay Kn otopian for platform access, and students access the platform using 
credentials provisioned by their institution.  
 
The embedded AI Interview module within the Student Portal is available to B2B students (Fina l 
year Students  only)  without separate subscription. The standalone B2C Interview Portal 
operates independently with its own subscription model (covered in a separate SOW).

6. Detailed Feature Spec ification  
6.1 Content Upload and AI Course Generation Pipeline  
The core content pipeline converts raw academic materials into structured AI -driven learning 
content.  
 
6.1.1 Content Ingestion  
• Supported input formats: PDF ,Text files, ques tion banks ,PPTs.  
• Content is tagged to a specific Chapter within the academic hierarchy  
• Content upload is performed  either  by Super Admin or authorised Faculty/SME  
 
6.1.2 RAG (Retrieval -Augmented Generation) Pipeline  
• End-to-end RAG pipeline converts uploaded documents i nto a structured knowledge base  
• Vector embeddings stored in Pinecone or Weaviate for semantic retrieval  
• Knowledge base is chapter -scoped to ensure content relevance during generation  
 
6.1.3 AI Avatar Video Course Generation  
• System generates an AI video course per chapter, using the chapter knowledge base . 
• Video generation is asynchronous , Faculty/SME is notified on completion . 
• Generated modules undergo SME r eview and approval before student publication  
• English is the primary language for video instruction . 
 
6.1.4 MCQ Generation  
• AI generates multiple -choice questions from chapter content  
• MCQs go through a Subject Matter Expert review and approval workflow befor e being 
activated for student assessments  
• Approved MCQs are tagged to subject and chapter for structured delivery

• Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) enforced across all three portal type s

9. AI Guardrails  
The AI system across all modules (QnA Tutor, Interview Engine, Course Generation) shall 
restrict generati on of  offensive, unsafe, or inappropriate content  
 
The system shall ensure all AI responses remain within the defined academic or interview 
context at all times.
