# DPDP Act 2023: technical gap list

Status values: **Implemented**, **Partial** or **Missing**. Evidence is in code, in tests (`tests/integration/test_privacy_*.py` in the backend) or in `DATA_INVENTORY.md`. Section numbers refer to the Act. Whether each duty applies, and how (for example whether the college or Quirri is the Data Fiduciary), is the owner's legal call.

| # | Duty | Status | Evidence | Gap / what would close it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Notice and consent (s.5, s.6): an itemised notice of the data and its purpose before processing, with consent recorded | **Missing** | No privacy notice, consent screen, terms link or consent record in `src/` (grep for "privacy", "consent", "terms" finds only CSS) or in the DB. Accounts are created by admins (`POST /api/v1/users`, bulk import) and activated through `/activate` with no notice | A notice at activation (login UI is frozen, so it goes on `/activate` or a first-login step), a `consents` record (user, notice version, time, withdrawn_at), and a notice in English and Tamil (s.5(3)). Alternatively, the college as fiduciary relies on "legitimate use" (s.7): an owner decision |
| 2 | Withdrawal of consent (s.6(4)) | **Missing** | No withdrawal path. Only per-event e-mail opt-outs (`notification_preferences`) | Withdrawal control that triggers erasure (row 6) |
| 3 | Purpose limitation and minimisation (s.4, s.6(1)) | **Partial** | The tutor prompt carries no identifiers; LiveKit sees only the UUID; video and MCQ prompts read no user (tests pass). Exports are role-scoped (tests pass) | The report preview JSON carries each student's email and id (the downloaded files do not); the interview-era `user_profiles` (gender, city, pincode) and `user_resumes` are kept with the module off; the free text the student types is sent to OpenAI unfiltered (a student can type their own phone number) |
| 4 | Right to access / summary (s.11) | **Partial** | STU can see their own profile (`/auth/me`), progress, quiz attempts and tutor history | No single "my data" summary or export, and no list of the processors their data went to (s.11(1)(b)) |
| 5 | Right to correction and completion (s.12(1)-(2)) | **Partial** | `PATCH /api/v1/auth/me` (first and last name, phone, avatar) | Email change is not implemented (schema note "STORY-03.5, not yet implemented"); academic placement is admin-only (reasonable, but there is no request path) |
| 6 | Right to erasure (s.12(3)) and erasure when purpose ends (s.8(7)) | **Missing** | No user delete route (`@router.delete` absent in `users.py`); deactivation is soft. A student can delete their own tutor conversation (`DELETE /voice_qna/conversations/{id}`) | Erasure / anonymisation job per user: users row, profile, resumes and S3 objects, watch progress, attempts, qna, notifications, deliveries, audit `actor_email` and `action` text; plus a rule for backups (expiry window) |
| 7 | Retention / storage limitation (s.8(7)) | **Partial** | Deleted-job files purged after 30 days (test); error events 30 d, health 35 d, dead tokens and throttles 1 d, read in-app alerts 90 d, one-time e-mail bodies never kept (test) | No limit for `audit_logs`, `notification_deliveries` (recipient addresses), `qna_turns`, `video_watch_progress`, `mcq_attempts`, deactivated users, the interview-era tables and S3 recordings; host backups keep 7, and off-site backup retention is not in the repo; the deleted-job row keeps `plan_json`, `change_note` and `source_filename` |
| 8 | Reasonable security safeguards (s.8(5)) | **Partial** | bcrypt passwords; SHA-256 refresh, reset and activation tokens; hashed throttle e-mail; jti-only denylist (tests). Deactivation or college closure ends every session: API, video, quiz, tutor cookie and voice token, refresh (tests). Ops log redaction of e-mail, phone, JWT, Bearer, `token=`, `password=` and path (PRIV-001, fixed). Off-site backups AES-256 | **PRIV-002**: the application log has recipient e-mails in clear. **PRIV-003**: `DEBUG=True` logs one-time links. Report downloads are not audit-logged (audit export is) |
| 9 | Personal data breach notification to the Board and each affected principal (s.8(6)) | **Missing** | No incident runbook, breach register or "notify affected users" path. Audit log and ops log exist (help with forensics) | Breach procedure (who, 72 h per the draft Rules), a template, and an ability to list affected users per tenant |
| 10 | Children's data (s.9): verifiable parental consent; no tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted ads | **Missing** (consent) / **Implemented** (no ads) | No DOB/age field, so minors cannot be identified; no guardian consent. No ads or third-party analytics found. The crisis signal is an anonymous per-college count (test: table has no person or text column; SA-only; log names no student) | Age or DOB capture at onboarding (or a college attestation), a guardian consent flow for under-18s, and a review of watch-progress and leaderboard as "behavioural monitoring" |
| 11 | Processors and contracts (s.8(2)) | **Partial** (technical) | Processors in code: OpenAI (tutor LLM, MCQ, video), Google Gemini (video), Sarvam (STT/TTS, India), AWS S3/SES (ap-south-1), LiveKit (self-hosted). Keys only in `.env` | No processor register or DPA references in the repo; vendor data-retention settings (for example OpenAI zero-retention or `store=false`) are not set in code (the request has no `store` field: default behaviour applies) |
| 12 | Cross-border transfer (s.16) | **Partial** | S3 and SES default to `ap-south-1`. The OpenAI and Gemini calls go outside India; the tutor prompt is identifier-free, but question text and voice-derived text leave India | Owner and legal decision on transfer under s.16 (allowed unless the country is notified as restricted); record it in the processor register |
| 13 | Grievance redressal and contact (s.8(10), s.13) | **Missing** | Support e-mail in the mail footer only | A published grievance contact / DPO in the app and notice |
| 14 | Significant Data Fiduciary duties (s.10), if notified | n/a | — | Owner decision |

## Defects registered

- **PRIV-001** (Fixed): ops error log redaction covered only e-mail and phone. JWTs, Bearer credentials, `?token=` links, echoed passwords and the request path were stored in the clear.
- **PRIV-002** (Blocked, INT owns `notification_service`): `deliver_email` logs `to=<address>` in plain text.
- **PRIV-003** (Blocked, INT): `DEBUG=True` logs the full body (reset and activation links) whatever the environment.
