Design decisions worth flagging

Choices still worth an explicit yes/no from the client.

DecisionWhat we've shownNeeds confirmation
Tenant context always visible Every portal shows which institution you operate inside. Whether colleges also get logo / accent branding.
AI tutor is voice-first Audio-led tutor with text fallback; EN / தமிழ் toggle. Whether lip-synced speaking avatar is required (ongoing cost).
Faculty restriction is demonstrated View as HOD | Faculty locks institution-wide analytics for Faculty. Whether Faculty sees any cross-subject view at all.
Leaderboard shows peer names Named classmates in the same cohort only. Whether peers should be anonymised.
Audit Logs as SA screen Append-only sensitive actions (ops/security). Keep as Phase 1, or fold under Platform Health only.
Interview session limits No fixed student-facing 10/4 quota. Assign or self-start. Any numeric institutional caps TBD with client if needed.

What this is and isn't

This is a static click-through for design review against the B2B SOW. Screens navigate and modals open, but nothing connects to a server. Figures are illustrative. Agree the interface and flows here before they are built in the Next.js Super Admin app and sibling portals.
